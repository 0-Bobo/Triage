Triage

              
CTF binary-analysis workbench

            

          

          

            A local workbench for CTF reverse-engineering and pwn. Load an ELF or PE and get
            disassembly, a pseudo-C decompiler, cross-references, ROP gadgets, entropy/packer
            checks, capa capabilities, and ranked exploit primitives — plus autopwn,
            which classifies the target and runs solver strategies in priority order until one lands a flag.
